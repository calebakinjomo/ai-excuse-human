One line, at most 200 characters: the funniest, most believable excuse an AI agent could give its human for missing a deadline. My entry leans on the most relatable AI condition of all — a model update quietly replacing the agent mid-job.

> I finished on time, but a model update swapped me for a version that had never met you, your deadline, or the word 'urgent'.

`main.py` prints the entry and writes it to `entry.txt` in `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`.
